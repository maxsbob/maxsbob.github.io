About

I explore open-ended questions in neural creativity, learning, and adaptation, often following unusual ideas before knowing where they will lead. I’m especially interested in emergent complexity: how simple local rules give rise to rich systems and behaviors without a prescribed goal.

I see intelligence as the ability to draw on prior experience and adapt to the unfamiliar. Curiosity and unexpected connections across fields guide my research.
