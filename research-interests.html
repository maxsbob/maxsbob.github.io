Research Interests

My current work focuses on creativity in neural networks, continual learning, and adaptation.

Neural creativity

What would it take for a neural network to develop an idea that is both original and useful? One question is how to recognize a creative response when there are many good answers.

Continual learning

How can a model learn from a sequence of experiences while preserving what it will need again?

Adaptation

When the environment changes, how can a model tell which parts of its earlier experience still apply?
